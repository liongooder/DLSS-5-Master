Sources for the Feeder components in payload\feeder and payload\feeder-original:

The original DLSS5-Feeder 2.0.0 ZIP and its unmodified binaries are included in
payload\feeder-original. The ZIP is pinned to the v2.0.0 GitHub release asset.

DLSS5-Feeder 2.0.0 (dlss5-feed.addon64, dlss5-feed.addon32, dlss5-feed-host64.exe,
  DLSS5_Feed.fx, Verify-DLSS5Feeder.ps1) - https://github.com/jlrouzies-fr/DLSS5-Feeder
  MIT; licence text in DLSS5-Feeder-LICENSE.txt. DLSS 5 Master builds the add-ons
  and helper with its in-game End panel and Vulkan recovery patches.

vort_Shaders, commit b410b9f0c0fbb83c8cb42164aaf1655fab386f4a (vort_Motion.fx, Includes\vort_*.fxh,
  Textures\vort_*.png) - https://github.com/vortigern11/vort_Shaders
  MIT; licence text in VORT-LICENSE.txt.

ReShade shader headers, commit ee30868391d4ad103db60489820102d8fd40e3c1 (ReShade.fxh, ReShadeUI.fxh)
  - https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders
  BSD-3-Clause; see ReShade-LICENSE.md in the DLSS 5 Master install folder's licenses folder.

The RenoDX add-ons used with the Feeder route are in payload\renodx and are listed in
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt in the DLSS 5 Master install folder.
