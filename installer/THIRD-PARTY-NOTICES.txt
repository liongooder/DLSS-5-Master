DLSS 5 Master
============

DLSS 5 Master installs and removes ReShade + RenoDX neural rendering routes
(Native, Multipass, Feeder), OptiScaler, and swaps upscaler DLLs in your games.

Every change it makes to a game is recorded in the "_DLSS5Master_Backup"
folder inside that game, and "Restore originals" puts the game back exactly
as it was. Uninstalling DLSS 5 Master does NOT restore your games - use
"Restore originals" on each game first if you want them back to stock.

Games with anti-cheat: injected DLLs can get you banned in online modes.
Use at your own risk.
